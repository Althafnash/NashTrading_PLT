# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 15, 2026**

Nash Trading ("Nash Trading", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Nash Trading website, applications, APIs, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

## 1. Information We Collect

Nash Trading is designed primarily as a market analytics and information platform. We aim to collect only information that is necessary to operate and improve the Services.

Depending on how you use the Services, information may include:

- Information you voluntarily provide to us.
- Technical information such as browser type, operating system, device information, and basic diagnostic information.
- IP address and other information that may be automatically provided by your browser or hosting provider.
- Information relating to API requests and application errors.
- Information stored locally within the application or device, where applicable.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific feature and you are informed about such collection.

## 2. Market Data

Nash Trading may display market and financial data obtained from third-party data providers.

Market data displayed through the Services is not personal information about you.

The availability, accuracy, timing, and completeness of market data may depend on the relevant third-party data provider.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information collected through the Services may be used to:

- Provide and operate the Services.
- Display and process market data.
- Maintain and improve the platform.
- Diagnose technical problems and errors.
- Protect the Services against abuse, unauthorized access, and security threats.
- Monitor platform performance.
- Comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of the Services unless permitted by applicable law or with appropriate notice or consent.

## 4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Nash Trading website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for the operation, security, analytics, or functionality of the website.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate information and choices regarding non-essential cookies.

You may also be able to control cookies through your browser settings.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Nash Trading may rely on third-party services to operate parts of the platform, including hosting, infrastructure, analytics, market-data providers, and other technical services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

## 6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the Services.

However, no website, application, server, or method of transmitting information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should therefore avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information through the Services unless the relevant feature specifically requests it.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including operating the Services, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and complying with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Services are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Nash Trading without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action where necessary.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which may include the right to:

- Request access to personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of certain information.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- Request information about how your information is processed.

These rights may be subject to legal limitations and exceptions.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the contact information provided on the Nash Trading website.

## 10. International Users

Nash Trading may use service providers or infrastructure located in countries other than the country in which you live.

As a result, information processed through the Services may be transferred to and processed in different jurisdictions, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the Services develop or as legal and regulatory requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the **Last Updated** date at the beginning of this document.

We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest version.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Nash Trading, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Nash Trading website.
